Our Story

Gauteng's Greatest Coffee 2025

Jozi Java Co (Pty) Ltd is a Johannesburg roastery, born at The Roastery in Douglasdale. We roast for the city we live in — energetic, unpretentious and proudly Jozi.

Bring the kids, bring your mates, bring the friend who reckons they don’t like coffee yet. Family-friendly isn’t a policy for us, it’s how we do business: the door is open, the welcome is warm and there’s always room at the table.

That spirit goes into every bag — The Wilds Roast, Pritchard Street Roast and Douglasdale Decaf, roasted in Douglasdale and sent out across Joburg, one fresh batch at a time.
