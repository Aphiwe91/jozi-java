Commercial Credit Solutions

Do You Qualify?

CCS arranges asset funded rental for equipment and conversions. It is an NCR registered credit provider (NCRCP13995). These are its three published starting criteria.

1+ Year

Registered & Trading

Your registered business has been trading for at least one year.

R1M+

Annual Turnover

Your business has annual turnover of at least R1 million.

VAT

VAT Registered

Your business is registered for VAT. All three criteria apply.

Meeting the starting criteria does not guarantee approval. CCS assesses the application and confirms rates, fees and agreement terms. Jozi Java does not provide credit.

Finance Questions

Is the deposit planner a rental quote?

No. It calculates a deposit and the remaining asset value only. Use CCS’s calculator for its indicative rental estimate. Only CCS can confirm the final rates, fees and agreement.

Does an enquiry mean I qualify?

No. The indication reflects your answers to the published starting criteria. CCS makes the final assessment and may request documents directly.

Can a new business buy equipment?

Talk to us about an outright purchase or a smaller starting setup. CCS’s criteria are for registered businesses trading for at least one year, with R1M+ annual turnover and VAT registration.
