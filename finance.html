Before You Start

Do You Qualify?

Asset finance for espresso machines, roasters and Tuk Tuk conversions is arranged through our finance partner, Commercial Credit Solutions (CCS) — ccs.equipment — an NCR-registered credit provider (NCRCP13995). Check the three criteria below before you configure anything: it saves time for buyers who do not yet qualify and sets expectations correctly.

1+ Year Trading

You must be trading as a registered business for at least one year.

R1M+ Turnover

Annual turnover of R1 million or more.

VAT Registered

Your business must be registered for VAT.

This page is a lead-qualification step, not a credit application. The actual finance agreement is entered into directly between you and CCS — Jozi Java simply pre-vets the enquiry and hands qualified leads across.
