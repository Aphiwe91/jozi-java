Our Story

Gauteng’s Greatest Coffee 2025

Jozi Java started in 2023 as a family business with a simple idea: Johannesburg deserves great coffee, roasted close to home and served with real care. Founded by Chris and Ruth Neumann, we roast our own blends at The Roastery in Douglasdale, from the rich, chocolatey Wilds Roast to the salted caramel notes of our Pritchard Street Roast. From our flagship café at Monte Circle to our coffee bars inside dealerships across Fourways, Sandton and Woodmead, we bring specialty coffee to the places Joburg lives and works. In 2025 we were proud to be named 702’s Gauteng’s Greatest Coffee Shop, but the real reward is still the same as day one: a great cup, a familiar face, and a city we love. This is The Coffee Experience.
