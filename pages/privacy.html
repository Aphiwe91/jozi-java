Who to contact

This website represents Jozi Java Co (Pty) Ltd. For questions about personal information, access, correction or deletion requests, contact info@jozijava.co.za or +27 68 923 5818. The team can direct a request to the responsible person.

What stays in your browser

The cart uses local storage on your device to remember coffee products, sizes, grinds and quantities. It does not store card details. Cart contents remain until you remove them, empty your bag or clear your browser’s site data.

Checkout remembers contact and delivery details only if you select the optional “Remember my details” box. Avoid this on a shared device. Use “Forget any saved details” at checkout or clear your browser’s site data to remove those saved details.

What happens when you enquire or order

Forms prepare a WhatsApp message on your device. The website does not have an email-submission service or a background customer database. A message is not received by Jozi Java until you send it in WhatsApp. Review its contents before sending.

If you send the message, Jozi Java receives the details you included to respond to your enquiry or process your order. WhatsApp, your email provider and any chosen payment provider handle information under their own terms and privacy notices. Jozi Java must confirm its business-wide retention arrangements and any further processing with you where required.

Third-party services

The contact map loads Google Maps only when you choose “Load Google Map”. External social, marketplace, directions and finance links take you to third-party websites. Those services may receive your IP address or other technical information when you connect to them.

This version of the website does not install marketing analytics, advertising pixels or newsletter-tracking scripts. Hosting providers may keep standard technical access logs. Adding payments, analytics or a subscription platform will require this notice to be reviewed.

Finance enquiries

The finance form asks for starting eligibility answers, not identity documents, bank statements or account numbers. The formal CCS application happens directly with CCS. Do not include sensitive documents or payment credentials in a website enquiry.

Marketing is a separate choice

Requesting an order, quote or finance discussion is not a marketing subscription. Asking about the Java Club starts a conversation with the team; it does not silently add you to an automated list.

Your rights
