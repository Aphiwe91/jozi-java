Orders, Delivery & Returns

Home/Orders, Delivery & Returns

1. Choose your coffee

Select a roast, bag size and grind. Check your cart before entering contact and delivery details. Bean prices currently shown are preview values awaiting client confirmation; they are not a final retail offer.

2. Send the prepared message

Checkout opens an order message in WhatsApp. Review it and press Send. The reference is generated in your browser; it is not proof of acceptance, payment or dispatch. Your cart remains saved so that a blocked window or an unsent message does not lose your order.

3. Confirm the quote before payment

Jozi Java confirms stock, final prices, delivery or collection, and payment instructions directly. This static website does not accept card payments, create a paid order or activate recurring billing. Never send card credentials or banking passwords through a message.

Courier & collection

The preview cart uses an illustrative R75 courier fee, with free courier from R500. These rates and the free delivery threshold await client approval. Your destination, stock availability and delivery terms must be confirmed by the team before payment. No delivery date is guaranteed by the cart.

Collection at The Roastery in Douglasdale is shown as free. Arrange the collection time and exact handover details with the team. South African delivery can be discussed; international checkout is not currently offered.

Equipment & conversions

Equipment prices are guide values from the supplied brief, including 15% VAT. Stock, current pricing, installation, delivery, manufacturer warranties and site requirements must be confirmed in a written quote. Imported equipment and Piaggio conversion prices may change.

A “from” vehicle or build price is not a confirmed complete coffee business package. Request an itemised quote before ordering. Finance terms are agreed with CCS and are subject to their assessment.

Damaged, incorrect or unwanted orders

Contact info@jozijava.co.za or +27 68 923 5818 with your order reference and a description of the issue. For damaged or incorrect items, photos of the packaging and item can help the team investigate. Ask the team before sending goods back so they can confirm the correct process.

Returns, cancellation, refund and warranty terms must be provided for your order before payment, consistent with applicable South African law. Nothing on this page limits your statutory consumer rights. This website does not invent a return window or blanket exclusion for coffee.

Final retail pricing, courier coverage, lead times and a client approved returns policy are still required before automated online sales can launch. For now, orders are confirmed personally with Jozi Java.
