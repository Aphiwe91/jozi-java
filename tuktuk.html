Frequently Asked Questions

What does a complete conversion cost?

It depends on the vehicle, fit out, machine, grinder, power and water solution. Ask for a current itemised quote. January 2026 Piaggio references are subject to change and do not confirm your package inclusions.

Can a new business get finance?

CCS’s published starting criteria require a registered business trading for 1+ year, annual turnover of R1M+ and VAT registration. A new venture may not meet them. Approval is subject to CCS’s assessment.

Can I trade anywhere?

No. You need to check site permission, local trading and health rules, insurance and vehicle requirements. A conversion does not automatically grant trading rights.

What should I send for a quote?

Your proposed site, expected peak demand, available power and water, budget and intended start date.
