The Partnership

A Turnkey Coffee Business on Three Wheels

Jozi Java has partnered with Piaggio Commercial SA to sell one all-in-one product: a Piaggio Ape Tuk Tuk, professionally converted into a coffee cart, with the machine, grinder, training and ongoing coffee supply included — financed as a single package through Commercial Credit Solutions (CCS).

The finance relationship happens quietly in the background with CCS. Leads are captured, described and pre-vetted right here on the Jozi Java website first, so when you enquire you are talking to a team that already knows the build, the machine and the numbers.

Every conversion package includes:

  • Piaggio Ape base vehicle, professionally converted into a coffee cart
  • Commercial espresso machine and grinder, specified for your build
  • Barista and operational training for you and your team
  • Ongoing coffee, bean and consumables supply from our Douglasdale roastery
  • One finance package covering vehicle, conversion and equipment through CCS
